Habit in Mind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Habit in Mind is a planner and habit tracker. It's built so your information stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put in the app stays on your device. That includes your schedule, tasks, countdowns, habits, routines, notes, files, photos, voice memos and settings. It also includes your profile (name, birthday and photo) and your app lock. We don't run servers that receive this data, and we can't see it.

Information we collect

Only what's needed to handle purchases (see below). Habit in Mind has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it never sends the things you put in the app to us or anyone else.

Purchases and the free trial

Habit in Mind is free to try for 7 days, then unlocks with a one-time purchase. Purchases are made through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), and we never see your payment details. To check what you've bought, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives a random ID made by the app, your purchase history (what, when, which store and the price) and technical details such as the app version, platform and country. It isn't linked to your name or email and isn't used for ads or tracking.

Talk-to-type and voice memos

When you use the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your phone's built-in speech recognition. When English is available offline on your phone, this happens on the device. Otherwise, your phone's system service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android) may process the audio under Apple's or Google's privacy policy. Habit in Mind never receives the audio. Voice memos you save are stored only on your phone.

Backups

You can make a backup file and save it wherever you choose, such as Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. The file then follows that service's terms, so keep it somewhere you trust. We never receive your backups.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN or password is stored as a secure hash in your phone's secure storage. Face ID or fingerprint unlocking is handled by your phone, and Habit in Mind never sees your biometric data.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. They aren't sent through a server.

Links you open

If you tap an address, phone number or website saved in an item, it opens in your maps, phone or browser app. That app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Habit in Mind isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. You can also delete individual items at any time. There's no account, so there's nothing to delete on a server. Your purchase history is kept by Apple or Google and RevenueCat so you can restore it on a new phone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
