The free trial and unlocking

Habit in Mind is free to try for 7 days. After that you can unlock it for good with a one-time purchase. There's no subscription and nothing is charged automatically. If you don't unlock it, the app becomes read-only: you can still see everything you've added and make a backup, but adding, editing and reminders stop until you unlock it.

Restoring a purchase

New phone or reinstalled the app? Open Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases. Your unlock comes back as long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Moving to a new phone

Everything you add stays on your phone, so it doesn't move by itself. On the old phone, open Settings → Data → Back up now and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email. On the new phone, open Settings → Data → Restore from a backup and choose that file.

Reminders aren't showing up

  • Make sure notifications are allowed for Habit in Mind in your phone's settings.
  • Check Settings → Notifications in the app, including quiet hours.
  • After the free trial, reminders pause until the app is unlocked.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are too: request one through the App Store (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play.
